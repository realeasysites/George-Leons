Origin story

It came from the North Shore

Logan Abbey grew up in Newburyport, Massachusetts, where a roast beef sandwich is part of everyday life. When he moved to Portland, the city's food scene was missing that beefy magic, so he set out to fix it.

It started in 2020 with pop-ups at breweries and restaurants, then the George's North Shore food truck rolled out that August. The Three Way built a cult following, and in 2023 the beef finally got a lair of its own at 9 Cumberland Street in Westbrook.

George is Logan's dog, a Chihuahua-pug mix named after the classically handsome George Costanza. Leon is Logan's son. Together they're the family business, and the family's a little weird. That's the point.
